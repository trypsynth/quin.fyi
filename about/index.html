About

I'm Quin (they/them), a nonbinary systems hacker and reverse engineer from the US. I'm fully blind minus light-perception and photophobia, and lost all my vision by the time I was 3.

By day I work at Pneuma Solutions. By night I work on Paperback, an accessible ebook and document reader, and whatever else I'm hyperfixated on that month. Lately that's been reverse engineering old software. My code is on GitHub.

This blog is mostly technical, but I've got opinions, and they'll leak out sometimes. By the standards of America's 70-plus-year-long McCarthyist hangover, I'm a commie libtard. Meanwhile in Europe I'd probably just vote for the Pirate Party.

Thanks for sticking around!
