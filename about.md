# About

I'm Quin (they/them), a nonbinary systems hacker and reverse engineer from the US. I'm fully blind minus light-perception and photophobia, and lost all my vision by the time I was 3.

By day I work at [Pneuma Solutions](https://pneumasolutions.com). By night I work on [Paperback](https://paperback.dev), an accessible ebook and document reader, and whatever else I'm hyperfixated on that month. Lately that's been reverse engineering old software. My code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/trypsynth).

Thanks for sticking around!
